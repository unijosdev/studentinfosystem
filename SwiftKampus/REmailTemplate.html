REGISTRATION PROCEDURE FOR FRESH UNDERGRADUATE STUDENTS FOR THE 2019/2020 ACADEMIC SESSION
  1. PRE-REGISTRATION/CHECKING ADMISSION STATUS
    1. Pre-Registration by students

      All fresh undergraduate students are to visit the portal https://portal.unijos.edu.ng and LOG IN to proceed with the registration. Use the email and password you provided during UTME/DE screening to login.


      Note: All candidates admitted in the University portal should print their admission letter from JAMB Portal. If a Candidate did not get admission letter, they should re-upload their SSCE results in JAMB portal.

      In addition, candidates whose admission in the University portal is a course other than the course they applied, should visit JAMB portal and accept the new course to enable the University propose their JAMB admission and process of their admission letter.


    2. Bio-data Registration

      In the personalized dashboard, select My Profile and then click Add/Edit Biodata. Fill and complete the bio-data and print five (5) copies and Proceed for Registration clearance.


  2. REGISTRATION/CLEARANCE PROCESS
    1. Central Registration Venues:

      Registration shall be conducted centrally in the respective Faculty Boardrooms located within the Faculty as follows:

      1. Faculty of Agriculture     -    Bauchi Road Campus
      2. Faculty of Arts    -    Permanent Site
      3. Faculty of Education    -    Permanent Site
      4. Faculty of Engineering    -    Permanent Site
      5. Faculty of Environmental Sciences     -    Permanent Site
      6. Faculty of Law     -    Bauchi Road Campus
      7. Faculty of Management Sciences     -    Permanent Site
      8. College of Health Sciences     -    Bauchi Road Campus
      9. Faculty of Natural Sciences     -    Bauchi Road Campus
      10. Faculty of Pharmaceutical Sciences     -    Bauchi Road Campus
      11. Faculty of Social Sciences     -    Permanent Site
      12. Faculty of Veterinary Sciences     -    Bauchi Road Campus


    2. Senate Affairs and Admissions Division Clearance (Central Registration)

      Meet the Central Registration Officers of their respective Faculties at the Registration Centre with the following:

      • Original and photocopies of credentials.
      • Five (5) copies of completed students’ Biodata forms.
      • JAMB Results/Registration Slip.
      • JAMB Admission Letter.
      • Birth Certificate/Sworn Declaration of Age.
      • Indigene Certificate or an affidavit from the Federal High Court.
      • WAEC/NECO/NABTEB scratch cards for results checking.
      • Affidavit to be of good conduct and non-membership of secret cult.


    3. Faculty Registration

      Meet the Faculty Registration Officer at the respective Faculties at the Registration Centre with the following:

      1. Clearance from the Central Registration Officer (Senate Affairs and Admissions Division).
      2. Same documents listed in (e) above.


    4. Payment of School charges.

      On successful clearance from the Central Registration Officer and Faculty Officer, proceed by paying the forty-five thousand naira (N45,000.00) school charges from personalized dashboard as follows:

      1. Select Fee Management and click on School Charges.
      2. Then select School Charges and Payment and follow the prompts.
      3. Print the payment receipt upon successful payment.


    5. Generation of Matriculation Number.

      Upon successful payment of school charges, matriculation number is automatically generated and student is provided with personalized University of Jos email.

      Fresh students should visit their dashboard to see and note their matriculation number


    6. Online Course Registration

      The fresh students proceed for online course registration on their Portal on payment for Identity Card.


    7. Verification of payment of School Charges

      Candidates present their school charges receipt to the Faculty Finance Officer (FFO) for verification. The FFO stamps and signs the receipt and returns same to the candidate.


    8. Departmental Registration

      Meet the Departmental Registration Officer/Level Coordinator at the appropriate Department with the following:

      1. Clearance from the Faculty Registration Officer and the Clearance copy from Central Registration Officer.
      2. Same documents listed in (e) above.
      3. Level Coordinator signed Clearance and five (5) copies of the Course Registration Form.
      Thereafter, students distribute each of the five copies as follows:
      • Departmental Registration Officer / Level Coordinators
      • Respective Faculty Officers
      • Senate Affairs and Admissions Division
      • Examinations and Records Division
      • Student’s Copy


    9. Hostel Accommodation

      Limited Hostel accommodation shall be allocated to all students (fresh and returning) who have paid all current (and outstanding school charges) on a first come first served bases upon payment of prescribed hostel accommodation fee. Interested students are to do the following:

      1. Pay N1,500 for Hostel Application Fee using their Portal.
      2. Select available Hostel and a Room will automatically be reserved for a period of twenty four (24) hours.
      3. Pay stipulated Hostel fee within the reserved period or risk revocation.
      4. Successful payment of Hostel allocation will be formally acknowledged.
      5. Proceed to Directorate of Students’ Affairs with payment Receipt for documentation.


    10. Registration in the Library

      Library registration is on-line on the University of Jos website. Print five (5) copies of the library registration form and bring to the library at Bauchi Road Campus for documentation.


    11. Registration at the University Health Services Centre

      Access to the University’s Health Services Registration Form is on-line. Note that Chest X-Ray and Laboratory results from only recognized government Hospital like the University Health Services, Teaching Hospitals, Federal Medical Centres, Specialist Hospitals are accepted.


    12. Students Identification Cards

      Identity Cards will be collected prior to commencement of lectures at the Security Office, Permanent Site after the completion of medical examination at the University Health Services Centre
